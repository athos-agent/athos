## [0:00–0:25] The hook: a new chat forgets

OPENING LINE (v2, required by the hackathon FAQ): small, over the first 6 s: «Built during the Meta Global AI
Developer Hackathon, Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026 · Pre-existing: an early sketch, rewritten this week». On screen in v2:
«A new chat forgets.» / «Close the tab: back to zero.» / «Your know-how adds up.» Meta's agents are listed first.

VISUAL: split screen. Left: a chat tab closes, the screen goes black, «Session reset». Right: a local folder,
`~/.athos`, lighting up page by page like a network that grows every day.

VOICE:
Everyone is waiting for super-intelligence like an event in the sky.
But the intelligence you use every day is rented. And rented intelligence has amnesia: close the tab, and it's back
to zero. Change tools, and everything you taught it stays behind.
Rented intelligence never adds up. Owned intelligence does.
That's why we built Athos.

## [0:25–0:55] What Athos is: your way of working, as plain files

VISUAL: the banner (musketeer + notebook). Then a page of `~/.athos/know-how/` as markdown, its steps lifting off
the page into a checklist. Logos of five agents around it: Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex, Muse Code, OpenCode.

VOICE:
Frontier models are engines anyone can rent. What makes the work yours is the route: your steps, your criteria,
every exception you learned the hard way.
Athos keeps that route as plain markdown on your own computer, and any agent can follow it: Claude, Codex,
OpenCode, and Meta's Muse Code on Muse Spark.

## [0:55–1:40] Live: your checklist, not tool calls

VISUAL: `demo/desktop-gallery-dd-en.gif` — in Claude Desktop, a due diligence of a fictional bakery chain in
Argentina bought by a Chilean fund. The panel opens: the finance team's steps, the legal team's steps, and
«↳ If the buyer is in another country → cross-border tax check», nested and ticking. Then
`demo/opencode-pii-en.gif`: «🔒 data through pii-guard».

VOICE:
Ask as you always do. Athos finds your way of working, and you watch the agent walk it: not tool calls, your own
checklist, opening a branch only when the case needs it.
Client data? A step can require pii-guard, which masks names and accounts before the model and restores them in the
answer. Numbers? Your steps say: compute them with code.

VISUAL: OpenCode palette → «Athos: change this step» → «add a column with the legal basis» → the table comes back
with the new column. Then «Save as a way of working».

VOICE:
And the golden rule: never teach the same thing twice. Change a step on the spot, or keep a whole task as a new
way of working, with one click. Every correction makes tomorrow's agent better.

## [1:40–2:05] Memory that stays in order

VISUAL: `demo/memory-en.gif` — an SRE team's journal, `athos curate` proposing a «don't» rule from a correction,

VOICE:
As your know-how grows, it stays in order. Each project keeps its own memory and its recent past, and Athos

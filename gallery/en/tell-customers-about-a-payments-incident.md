title: Tell customers about a payments incident
when: an incident affects payments (checkout, payouts, billing) and customers, support and finance must be told what is happening
profession: ops
lang: en
---

## #first_notice
**When:** within 30 minutes of confirming that payments are affected (the team's target; replace it with yours).
**Do:** draft the status page notice saying what customers can't do, since when, whether money is at risk (double charges, late payouts) and when the next update comes. No cause and no blame yet. The person approves it.
**Because:** a short, honest first notice prevents a flood of tickets and chargebacks.
**Links:** [[tell-customers-about-a-payments-incident#support]]

## #support
**When:** the notice is approved.
**Do:** give support a reply with the same words, and finance the window, provider and amounts of the affected transactions to reconcile, with no personal data in the channel.
**Links:** [[tell-customers-about-a-payments-incident#updates]]

## #updates
**When:** until it is resolved.
**Do:** update the status page at the promised time even when nothing changed, and close it saying what happened to pending payments.
**Because:** silence after a notice reads as worse news.

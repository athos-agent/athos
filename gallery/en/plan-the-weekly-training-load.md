## #load
**When:** with the training log.
**Do:** compute with code the load of each session (session effort 1-10 × minutes), the acute load (last 7 days), the chronic load (average weekly load of the last 4 weeks) and the acute:chronic ratio.
**Because:** arithmetic is done with code, and the ratio says more than this week's total.
**Links:** [[plan-the-weekly-training-load#ratio]]

## #ratio
**When:** the ratio is known.
**Do:** compare it with the coach's thresholds (for example a target band of 0.8 to 1.3; replace it with yours).
**If:** acute:chronic load > 1.3 → [[plan-a-deload-week]]
**Because:** a sudden rise in load over the usual is when overuse injuries pile up; the coach sets the exact threshold.
**Links:** [[plan-the-weekly-training-load#sessions]]

## #sessions
**When:** the target load for the week is set.
**Do:** lay out the sessions day by day, with hard sessions at least 48 hours apart, the match or test day protected, the week's load within 10% of the target.
**Links:** [[plan-the-weekly-training-load#check_in]]

## #check_in
**When:** the plan is ready.
**Do:** list what to ask the athlete on Monday (sleep, soreness, mood, other commitments) that would change the plan. The coach decides.

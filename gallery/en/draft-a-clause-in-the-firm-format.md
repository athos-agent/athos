## #defined_terms
**When:** before writing.
**Do:** reuse the contract's defined terms exactly as defined (capitalised); define a new term only once, in the clause that first needs it.
**Links:** [[draft-a-clause-in-the-firm-format#structure]]

## #structure
**When:** writing.
**Do:** number the clause and its sub-clauses (5, 5.1, 5.1.1), one obligation per sub-clause, the obliged party as the subject, "shall" for duties and "may" for rights, sentences under 40 words.
**Because:** one obligation per sub-clause can be cited, negotiated and breached one at a time.
**Links:** [[draft-a-clause-in-the-firm-format#redline]]

## #redline
**When:** the clause is written.
**Do:** show it as a redline against the original text and, under it, one line per change saying why, for the other side.
**Because:** the other side accepts faster what comes explained.

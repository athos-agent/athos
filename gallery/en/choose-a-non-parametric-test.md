## #pick
**When:** the design is known.
**Do:** pick the test by the design. Two independent groups → Mann-Whitney U; two paired measures → Wilcoxon signed-rank; three or more groups → Kruskal-Wallis, then Dunn's test with a correction for multiple comparisons.
**Links:** [[choose-a-non-parametric-test#effect]]

## #effect
**When:** the test has run.
**Do:** report the matching effect size (rank-biserial correlation, or epsilon squared) and the medians with their interquartile ranges.
**Links:** [[choose-a-non-parametric-test#methods]]

## #methods
**When:** writing the methods.
**Do:** say why a non-parametric test was used: n per group, and which assumption failed.
**Because:** a reviewer's first question about a small sample is why this test.

## #statements
**When:** with the statements at hand.
**Do:** gather three years of balance sheet, income statement and cash flow; note who audited them, or that nobody did.
**Links:** [[read-the-financials-of-an-sme#adjust]]

## #adjust
**When:** the three years are there.
**Do:** normalise the figures with code (the owner's salary and personal expenses paid by the company, one-off items, transactions with related parties) and restate in a hard currency with each year's exchange rate when inflation is high.
**Because:** in a high-inflation economy, nominal growth can hide a real decline.
**Links:** [[read-the-financials-of-an-sme#cash]]

## #cash
**When:** the figures are normalised.
**Do:** work out the working capital, every debt (banks, tax payment plans, loans from the owners) and how many months of cash are left.
**Because:** small companies rarely fail on profit; they fail on cash.

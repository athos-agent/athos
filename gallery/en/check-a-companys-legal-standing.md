## #registry
**When:** at the start.
**Do:** read the company registry for the incorporation, bylaws, current directors and shareholders, and the powers of whoever signs.
**Links:** [[check-a-companys-legal-standing#contingencies]]

## #contingencies
**When:** the company's structure is clear.
**Do:** list lawsuits, labour claims, tax debts and payment plans and guarantees given, from official certificates, not from the seller's word.
**Because:** in many countries labour and tax liabilities stay with the company, and so pass to the buyer.
**Links:** [[check-a-companys-legal-standing#contracts]]

## #contracts
**When:** the contingencies are listed.
**Do:** review the key contracts (clients, suppliers, leases), with any change-of-control clause and their expiry dates.
**Because:** a change-of-control clause can let a key client walk away the day the sale closes.

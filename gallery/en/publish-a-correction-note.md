## #what_was_wrong
**When:** the error is confirmed.
**Do:** state what was published, what is correct and when the story was changed, with no euphemisms ("an earlier version of this story said…").
**Links:** [[publish-a-correction-note#where]]

## #where
**When:** the note is written.
**Do:** put the note at the end of the story, fix the story itself, and correct the social posts that spread the error.
**Links:** [[publish-a-correction-note#log]]

## #log
**When:** the correction is out.
**Do:** add it to the newsroom's corrections log with its cause (source, reporting, editing), so patterns show.
**Because:** one correction is a mistake; three from the same cause are a process to fix.

title: Add a data protection clause to a services contract
when: a provider will process personal data on a client's behalf and the services contract needs a data processing clause
profession: law
lang: en
---

## #roles
**When:** at the start.
**Do:** state who decides why and how the data is processed (the client) and who only processes it on instructions (the provider), which data it covers and whose.
**Links:** [[add-a-data-protection-clause#duties]]

## #duties
**When:** the roles are clear.
**Do:** list the provider's duties: process only on written instructions, confidentiality of its staff, security measures, notice of a breach within an agreed number of hours (example: 48), sub-processors only with consent, deletion or return of the data at the end, and the audits the client may run. Ask the person which law applies.
**Because:** these are the points data protection laws usually require of a processor; the exact list depends on the jurisdiction.
**Links:** [[add-a-data-protection-clause#draft]]

## #draft
**When:** the duties are listed.
**Do:** write the clause.
**Calls:** [[draft-a-clause-in-the-firm-format]]
**Because:** the firm's clauses read the same in every contract, so the other side reviews them faster.

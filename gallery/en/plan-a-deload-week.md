## #cut_volume
**When:** a deload is needed.
**Do:** cut the week's volume by 40 to 50% and keep intensity only in short blocks.
**Because:** volume drives fatigue; short intense blocks keep the athlete sharp.
**Links:** [[plan-a-deload-week#keep]]

## #keep
**When:** laying out the sessions.
**Do:** keep one quality session and the technical work; replace the rest with easy sessions and mobility.
**Links:** [[plan-a-deload-week#back]]

## #back
**When:** the week ends.
**Do:** compute the ratio again and return to the usual load only once it is back in the target band.

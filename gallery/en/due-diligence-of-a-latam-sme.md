## #scope
**When:** the seller's documents arrive.
**Do:** write what is being bought (shares or assets), the country, the size (revenue, staff) and which documents were received; say what is missing.
**Links:** [[due-diligence-of-a-latam-sme#financials]]

## #financials
**When:** the scope is clear.
**Do:** read the financial statements the way the finance team does.
**Calls:** [[read-the-financials-of-an-sme]]
**Links:** [[due-diligence-of-a-latam-sme#legal]]

## #legal
**When:** alongside the financials.
**Do:** check the company's legal standing the way the legal team does.
**Calls:** [[check-a-companys-legal-standing]]
**Links:** [[due-diligence-of-a-latam-sme#borders]]

## #borders
**When:** the shareholders and the buyer are known.
**Do:** note where the buyer, the company and each shareholder are resident for tax.
**If:** the buyer or a shareholder is in another country → [[check-the-cross-border-tax-treatment]]
**Because:** the price that matters is net of the taxes on the sale and on future dividends, which change across borders.
**Links:** [[due-diligence-of-a-latam-sme#memo]]

## #memo
**When:** every area is reviewed.
**Do:** write one memo with the findings by area, red flags, questions for the seller and what would change the price. Leave the offer to the person.
**Because:** each specialist keeps their own page; the memo is where their findings meet.

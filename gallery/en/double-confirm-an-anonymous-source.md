title: Double-confirm an anonymous source
when: a story relies on a source who will not be named
profession: journalism
lang: en
---

## #second_source
**When:** before the anonymous claim goes in the story.
**Do:** find a second, independent source (one the first didn't tell) or a document that confirms the key fact.
**Because:** two people who heard it from the same place are one source.
**Links:** [[double-confirm-an-anonymous-source#editor]]

## #editor
**When:** the fact is confirmed twice.
**Do:** tell the editor who the source is and why anonymity is justified (a risk to their job or safety); the editor approves it.
**Links:** [[double-confirm-an-anonymous-source#wording]]

## #wording
**When:** writing.
**Do:** describe the source as precisely as possible without identifying them ("an official in the ministry's procurement office") and say why they asked not to be named.

## #read_side
**When:** a contract to review arrives with the side the person acts for.
**Do:** note the side and review every clause from it; skip the confidentiality question when the person says the data is fictitious.
**Because:** the same clause is good for one side and bad for the other.
**Links:** [[review-a-contract#checklist]]

## #checklist
**When:** reading the contract.
**Do:** check parties, scope, term, price and payment, governing law and jurisdiction (say which is missing); deposit or security and its return; early termination and notice for both sides (flag it if one-sided); days to pay and late interest; liability always capped, never unlimited; automatic renewal, assignment and jurisdiction.
**Because:** the person's own checklist (my-checklist.md).
**Links:** [[review-a-contract#table]]

## #table
**When:** every point is checked.
**Do:** write review.md with a table: clause, issue, proposed wording, priority (must / should / minor). A missing governing-law clause is a row of its own, priority must.
**Because:** the person's format; the governing-law row was their correction.
**Links:** [[review-a-contract#push_first]]

## #push_first
**When:** the table is done.
**Do:** end with a line `Push first:` naming the three clauses to negotiate first, and say the decision stays with the client. Never decide for the client.
**Because:** the person's correction while teaching.

## #read_chapter
**When:** a chapter of the author's novel arrives to be edited.
**Do:** edit it into `<chapter>-edited.md` and list every change with its reason in `edits.md`; never delete a paragraph: suggest cuts in edits.md.
**Because:** cutting is the author's decision.
**Links:** [[edit-a-chapter#style]]

## #style
**When:** editing the narration and the dialogue.
**Do:** narration in the past tense; dialogue in double quotes, never dashes; never change what a character says (grammar included); dialogue tags without adverbs; numbers under one hundred in words; the heroine is Ilse, never Ilsa.
**Because:** the author's style guide (style-guide.md).
**Links:** [[edit-a-chapter#water_and_spelling]]

## #water_and_spelling
**When:** every chapter.
**Do:** the sea is always "the water", never "the sea" or "the ocean"; British spelling throughout (colour, grey, harbour).
**Because:** the author's two standing rules, added while teaching.

title: Plan the training week with my rules
when: planning next week's training for an athlete or a team from their session log (minutes, effort, type), their calendar and availability
---

## #coach_rules
**When:** before planning.
**Do:** use MY rules, not generic ones. These are examples, to be replaced with your own: session load = minutes × perceived effort (RPE 1–10); never more than +10% a week; after a spike week, cut back.
**Because:** every coach has a method; a plan with invented rules is not theirs.
**Links:** [[plan-the-training-week#loads]]

## #loads
**When:** with the log of the last four weeks.
**Do:** compute with code (not by eye) each week's load (Monday to Sunday) and the acute:chronic ratio = last week's load ÷ the mean of the four. Show the numbers.
**Because:** arithmetic is done with code; judgment, with the rules.
**Links:** [[plan-the-training-week#target]]

## #target
**When:** the loads are computed.
**Do:** next week's target = last week × 0.90 if the ratio is above 1.3; otherwise × 1.10.
**Links:** [[plan-the-training-week#sessions]]

## #sessions
**When:** with the target load.
**Do:** build the sessions so they add up to the target (±5%): at most two hard days (RPE 7 or more), never consecutive; one long session and the day after it easy (RPE 3 or less) or rest; no session on days the athlete can't train.
**Links:** [[plan-the-training-week#decisions]]

## #decisions
**When:** the plan is built.
**Do:** list as open decisions what the athlete or coach decides (whether to run a tune-up race, the goal pace) and keep them out of the plan. If there is pain or injury, refer to a health professional; never plan around it.
**Because:** the plan proposes; decisions belong to whoever coaches and whoever competes.

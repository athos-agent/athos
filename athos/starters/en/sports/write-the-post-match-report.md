title: Write the post-match report in my format
when: after a match or competition, preparing the match report from stats, notes or video, in the coach's format
---

## #data
**When:** with the match stats or notes.
**Do:** compute with code what can be counted (possession, shots, errors, minutes per player) and cite where each number comes from. What is not in the data is not invented.
**Links:** [[write-the-post-match-report#three_things]]

## #three_things
**When:** the numbers are in.
**Do:** write in the coach's format: three things that went well, three to fix (each with its moment or number) and one focus for next week. Direct tone, never blaming players by name in the team report.
**Because:** a short, concrete report gets read; a long one doesn't.
**Links:** [[write-the-post-match-report#decisions]]

## #decisions
**When:** the report is ready.
**Do:** selection, position changes or sanctions are the coach's decisions: propose them as questions, never decide them.

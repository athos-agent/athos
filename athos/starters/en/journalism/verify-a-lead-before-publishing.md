title: Verify a lead before publishing it
when: checking a tip, a leak or a claim before publishing: public contracts, official gazettes, company records, documents from a source; or fact-checking a statement
---

## #protect_the_source
**When:** before pasting or opening any document from a source.
**Do:** ask the person whether it can reveal who the source is (names, metadata, internal identifiers). If it can, propose removing them first, running the task through [[connector:pii-guard]], or a local model (`athos delegate` with Ollama).
**Because:** a hosted model sees everything shown to it; protecting the source is the journalist's duty, and it comes first.
**Links:** [[verify-a-lead-before-publishing#claims]]

## #claims
**When:** with the material at hand.
**Do:** break the lead into checkable claims, one per line: who, what, when, how much. Mark which ones the story depends on.
**Because:** a story is as strong as its weakest essential claim.
**Links:** [[verify-a-lead-before-publishing#two_sources]]

## #two_sources
**When:** for each essential claim.
**Do:** find two independent sources (a public record and a person, or two records from different bodies). Two articles that copy each other are one source. Write each source with its date and where it can be checked.
**Because:** the double independent source is the rule; one source is a lead, not a fact.
**Links:** [[verify-a-lead-before-publishing#dates_and_links]]

## #dates_and_links
**When:** the story involves companies, public money or officials.
**Do:** cross the dates: when each company was founded and by whom, when the contract was awarded, when officials took office. Look for family or business links between the parties in the company registry and the gazette. Every new trap you find (a shell company founded days before a tender, a front partner) becomes a step of this way of working.
**Because:** money is hidden in the order of dates and in who stands behind whom.
**Links:** [[verify-a-lead-before-publishing#right_of_reply]]

## #right_of_reply
**When:** the claims hold.
**Do:** list who is accused of what and draft the questions to ask each one for their reply, with a deadline. Publishing, and what to publish, is the person's and their editor's decision.
**Because:** fairness and the law require asking before publishing.

id: check-the-cross-border-tax-treatment
title: Check the cross-border tax treatment of an investment
when: an investment, income or vehicle involves more than one country (investor, asset or company abroad) and its taxes, withholdings and double-taxation treaty must be checked
---

## #residences
**When:** at the start.
**Do:** state the tax residence of the investor and the country of the asset and of each vehicle in between. Ask the person when any of them is unclear.
**Links:** [[check-the-cross-border-tax-treatment#treaty]]

## #treaty
**When:** the countries are known.
**Do:** check whether a double-taxation treaty applies between each pair, and which article covers this income (dividends, interest, capital gains, real estate). Cite the treaty and article, from the official text.
**Because:** the treaty, not the general rate, sets the withholding.
**Links:** [[check-the-cross-border-tax-treatment#withholding]]

## #withholding
**When:** with the treaty.
**Do:** a table per flow: withholding at source, tax at residence, credit available, net rate. Mark every figure that must be confirmed with a local advisor.
**Because:** a rate copied from memory is the most common error in cross-border analysis.

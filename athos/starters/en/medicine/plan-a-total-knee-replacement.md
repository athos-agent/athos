---
id: plan-a-total-knee-replacement
title: Organise the planning of a total knee replacement
when: preparing or reviewing the preoperative plan of a total knee arthroplasty (TKA) from imaging and the clinical history, for the surgeon to decide and sign
---

## #scope
**When:** at the start.
**Do:** state that this organises the surgeon's work and is not medical advice: every clinical decision (indication, alignment, implant, approach) is made and signed by the surgeon. If the model is asked to decide something, return it as a question.
**Because:** responsibility lies with the professional; a plan that decides on its own is a risk.
**Links:** [[plan-a-total-knee-replacement#patient_data]]

## #patient_data
**When:** before showing images or history.
**Do:** use anonymised studies (no name, ID or birth date in the images or DICOM headers) or a local model. Ask the person before opening any file with identifiable data.
**Because:** health data must not leave the machine without need or consent.
**Links:** [[plan-a-total-knee-replacement#studies]]

## #studies
**When:** with the studies at hand.
**Do:** list what is there and what is missing to plan: weight-bearing X-rays (AP, lateral, patellar axial), full-length standing alignment film and, if the surgeon plans in 3D, the CT. Flag missing or poor studies instead of assuming measurements.
**Links:** [[plan-a-total-knee-replacement#measurements]]

## #measurements
**When:** the studies are enough.
**Do:** record each measurement with its source (study, date, who measured): mechanical axis, varus/valgus deformity, tibial slope, bone defects, patellar condition. Every number with its origin; if a measurement cannot be made, say so.
**Because:** a number without a source cannot be checked in the operating room.
**Links:** [[plan-a-total-knee-replacement#decisions]]

## #decisions
**When:** the measurements are in.
**Do:** build the list of decisions the surgeon must make, each with its options and the data behind it: alignment strategy, cuts, implant size, defect management (augments, graft), patellar treatment, expected ligament balance. Do not choose: present.
**Because:** separating what was measured from what was decided is what makes the plan reviewable.
**Links:** [[plan-a-total-knee-replacement#final_plan]]

## #final_plan
**When:** the surgeon has decided.
**Do:** write the plan in the surgeon's format: decisions with their reasons, implants and alternative sizes to have available, critical steps and what to check in the operating room. Mark any point the surgeon left open.

id: summarise-a-referral
title: Summarise a referral or clinical history for the visit
when: preparing a visit from a referral letter, a discharge summary or previous studies; summarising a patient's history or a colleague's letter
---

## #patient_data
**When:** before opening the documents.
**Do:** confirm with the person whether the documents hold identifiable data; if they do, anonymise them or use a local model.
**Because:** health data must not leave the machine without need.
**Links:** [[summarise-a-referral#summary]]

## #summary
**When:** with the documents at hand.
**Do:** summarise in the person's format: reason for referral, relevant history, medication and allergies, studies with date and result, and the referring colleague's exact question. Every item with the document it comes from.
**Because:** an item without a source cannot be checked in the visit.
**Links:** [[summarise-a-referral#gaps]]

## #gaps
**When:** the summary is done.
**Do:** list what is missing or does not match across documents (dates, doses, results) and what to ask the patient. Never fill gaps with guesses; clinical judgment is the person's.

title: Review a contract against your checklist
when: reviewing, comparing or commenting a contract (lease, services, sale, NDA, employment) before signing it or sending it back with comments
---

## #data_out
**When:** before pasting or opening the contract.
**Do:** confirm with the person whether it holds client data that must not leave their machine; if so, propose anonymising names and amounts, or a local model (`athos delegate` with Ollama).
**Because:** a cloud model sees everything shown to it; professional secrecy is the person's duty.
**Links:** [[review-a-contract-against-your-checklist#parties_and_object]]

## #parties_and_object
**When:** with the contract at hand.
**Do:** summarise in five lines: parties, object, term, price and payment, governing law and jurisdiction. Say if any is missing.
**Links:** [[review-a-contract-against-your-checklist#key_clauses]]

## #key_clauses
**When:** the summary is done.
**Do:** go clause by clause, citing its number: termination and notice, penalties, limitation of liability, indemnity, confidentiality, assignment, automatic renewal, dispute resolution. Mark each as for, neutral or against the party the person represents.
**Because:** every comment must point to the text; without a clause number it cannot be discussed.
**Links:** [[review-a-contract-against-your-checklist#comments]]

## #comments
**When:** the clauses are marked.
**Do:** return a table: clause, issue, proposed wording, priority (must / should / minor). What to negotiate is the person's decision; never present the proposal as final.
**Because:** professional judgment and responsibility belong to whoever signs the review.
